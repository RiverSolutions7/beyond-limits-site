Stamford Peace Basketball League
The Peace Basketball League for 1st thru 8th graders offers high level basketball instruction, well-balanced competition, organized and thoughtful scheduling, energetic and engaged coaches/referees and great fun! Teams will be grade*-based.
*Grades may be combined based on numbers.

One practice one evening a week and one game on the weekend at a local gym.

Player Evaluations
Evaluations will be held for ALL players in 1st through 8th grade.
Players in 7th & 8th grade, NBA division, will be drafted by our volunteer coaches.
All players (new and returning) in 7th and 8th grade should attend the Sunday, November 2nd Evaluation.
*Players only need to attend one evaluation. All players will be placed on teams.
Saturday, Nov. 1 and Sunday, Nov. 2
Yerwood Center &bull; 90 Fairfield Avenue, Stamford
Evaluation times are as follows:
1st & 2nd Grade &bull; 9 - 9:45 a.m.
3rd & 4th Grade &bull; 10 - 10:45 a.m.
5th & 6th Grade &bull; 11 – 11:45 a.m.
7th & 8th Grade &bull; 12 – 12:45 p.m.
Date/Times/Location Subject To Change
