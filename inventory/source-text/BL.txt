    Our Program

    
      Launched in 2014 as a program of the Stamford Peace Youth Foundation, the Beyond Limits Academic Program provides highly subsidized one-on-one tutoring and informal mentoring to students attending Stamford Public Schools and other schools in lower Fairfield County. Tutoring is typically conducted by upperclassmen, many of whom are referred by teachers, administrators and other tutors. We also have a number of college students around the world working with us. Tutoring sessions are held at our headquarters on Long Ridge Road, which includes dedicated tutoring rooms equipped with chromebooks, school supplies, and other program materials, a lounge area with Wi-Fi for families, a kitchen with water and snacks, and academic reference books. Please click HERE to view our most recent program highlights. 

    

  

                
  
      
  
    Vision

    
      To create an equitable academic ecosystem in lower Fairfield County and beyond by empowering every learner through tutoring, informal mentoring, advocacy, and collaboration with local partners.

    

  

                
  
      
  
    Mission

    
      Beyond Limits' mission is to level the academic playing field and eliminate opportunity gaps for learners in lower Fairfield County. We empower every learner to achieve their potential through high-quality, subsidized, non-school-based peer-to-peer tutoring, mentoring, academic advocacy, and enrichment activities. By collaborating with local partners, we create a sustainable, inclusive academic support network that fosters a thirst for knowledge and equitable access to opportunities.

    Core values

    
      

	
	EQUITY through access: Providing accessible, discounted, high-quality academic supports to bridge the opportunity gap among all participants
	
	
	EMPOWERMENT through support: Fostering confidence through personalized tutoring, enrichment programming and informal mentoring to help students reach their full potential. 
	
	
	COMMUNITY through collaboration: Building a support network among students, families, tutors and other community organizations to create a broad and supportive learning environment that drives success.

    The Stamford Rotary Trust Scholarship

This scholarship is awarded to Beyond Limits students who are in good academic standing or show commitment to improve that standing, displays good citizenship, and demonstrates financial need.

    Bill Susetka Memorial Scholarship

    
      This scholarship is awarded to students in honor of Bill Susetka's memory. Bill graduated from Stamford Catholic High School. He was accomplished in athletics, having played on the Stamford World Champion Babe Ruth baseball team and the varsity baseball and basketball teams at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. Still, he remained humble and reveled in other’s triumphs. Bill was an inspirational leader with a kind, compassionate, and generous nature, who cared deeply about people and always had a smile and encouraging words to share. To honor his memory, the friends and family of Bill Susetka have established this scholarship for worthy Stamford Peace student-athletes. The successful candidate is in good academic standing, displays good citizenship, and demonstrates financial need.

    ACADEMIC TUTORING

    
      
	
	We provide Individualized academic tutoring in math & science – generally two 1-hour sessions per week per participant, available on weekday afternoons and evenings and Saturdays on a personalized schedule.
	
	
	Tutoring is conducted “peer-to-peer”, i.e., our tutors are high-achieving local high school juniors, seniors, and local college students assigned to 4th thru 10th graders. Tutors and tutees rotate, with pairings based upon our assessment of current best “fit.”
	
	
	Tutoring sessions can take place in person in our tutoring center or remotely via Zoom, which can allow for greater flexibility and convenience. 
	
	
	Tutoring fees are on a sliding scale and are substantially subsidized for families eligible to receive discounted services (formerly free or reduced-price school lunch).
	
	
	Our approach also offers valuable informal mentoring benefits.

    Enrichment Programming

    
      Our enrichment and summer programs supplement our regular tutoring and academic intervention programming. To receive updates on program offerings, please follow us on social media.

We offer enrichment activities on a variety of topics, including:

	Computer coding workshops;
	Writing workshops;
	College planning workshops;
	Time management and organizational skills workshops;
	And more!

    

  

        
          
                
  
      
  
    SUMMER PROGRAMMING

    
      Our current summer programming includes:

	On-site and remote academic tutoring; and
	Our 1-week middle school preparatory academy is designed to help orient incoming 6th graders (Middle School Summer Scholars Program), which includes time management, organization, note taking, study skills tutorials, and much more.
