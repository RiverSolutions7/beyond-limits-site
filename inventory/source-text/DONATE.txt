Give Through Your Donor-Advised Fund (DAF)

Stamford Peace Youth Foundation is grateful to accept gifts through Donor-Advised Funds.

What is a Donor-Advised Fund (DAF)

A donor-advised fund is a charitable giving account that allows you to make a donation, receive an immediate tax deduction, and then recommend grants to nonprofits over time. Many donors use DAFs to simplify and manage their charitable giving.

How do I make a DAF gift online?

Contact your DAF manager (such as Fidelity Charitable, Schwab Charitable, Vanguard, etc.) and recommend a grant to:​​

Stamford Peace Youth Foundation, Inc.
EIN: 27-1254631

​​What information will I need?

Most providers will ask for:

Our legal name: Stamford Peace Youth Foundation

Our EIN: 27-1254631

Our mailing address: 925 Long Ridge Road, Stamford, CT 06902

Optional: Program or fund designation

Will I receive a tax receipt for my DAF gift?

You will not receive a tax receipt from us for a DAF gift, because you receive your tax deduction when contributing to your DAF. However, we always send an acknowledgement letter to express our gratitude as long as you provide us with the information to do so.

Who should I contact with questions?

You can reach our team anytime at info@peaceyouthct.org for help with DAF gifts or donor questions. We are happy to assist!

Bill Susetka Memorial Scholarship Fund

Bill Susetka was a student-athlete at Stamford's Catholic High School and later at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, playing basketball and baseball. He was inducted into the RPI Hall of Fame and was a Collegiate Scholar Athlete. After starting his career at Clairol, Inc. in Stamford, he retired as President of the company. He was known buy his peers as being the commensurate leader and mentor. It is fitting that this scholarship reflects his passion for sports, leadership, and mentoring.

Please make your donation to the Bill Susetka Memorial Schlolarship Fund HERE - and select "Bill Susetka Memorial Scholarship Fund" from the CAMPAIGN drop down box.

Kosovo Heritage Basketball Academy

The KHBA’s mission is to educate, empower, and inspire young people in Kosovo using the game of basketball as a tool to develop their love for the game; their skills and talents; and their dreams and opportunities for a brighter future. The idea is to grow a community of leaders through more advanced coaching and mentorship that will enrich each participant’s athletic skills and leadership capabilities, and further their education through basketball. Through these efforts, we hope to enhance the quality and reach of Kosovo basketball for the long-term.

Please make your donation to the Kosovo Heritage Basketball Academy HERE - and select "KHBA" from the CAMPAIGN drop down box.
