Stamford Peace Youth Foundation

Peace Basketball

Three leagues, from a first season of developmental basketball to a nationally competitive travel program. Beyond Limits Academics is the Foundation’s tutoring program and runs alongside them.

Three ways to play

Season dates, fees and tryout schedules change every year, so each league links to the page where its own are kept current.

Peace AAU

Our elite-level AAU basketball program, with teams for boys and girls in the 3rd through 11th grades. Tryout based, teams are both competitive and very selective. Each team plays between 5-12 tournaments per season (depending upon grade and level of play) throughout the Northeast region and elsewhere. Tryouts for our AAU teams take place towards the end of the winter season.

Go to the AAU page

Stamford Peace Boys Basketball League

The Stamford Peace Boys Basketball League, a local winter developmental league (available without regard to basketball ability or experience) for elementary and middle school boys. This league generally runs from mid-November through early March. Teams practice one weekday per week, followed by an either Saturday or Sunday game. Every team earns a berth in a single-elimination playoff/championship tournament.

Run with the Stamford JCC, which will accommodate Sabbath observers with Sunday only scheduling. JCC members receive 10% off the registration price.

Summer League

Peace Basketball Summer League, as a program of J Basketball is a summer league (available without regard to basketball ability or experience) for middle school boys and high school boys. This league runs from the July timeframe through mid-August. All teams have 2 games games per weekend and concludes with a two day-single tournament.

Academics come first

Peace Basketball is deeply concerned with the academic performance and conduct of its players. As a condition to playing with Peace, participants are required to submit a copy of their report card or other academic progress reports, which are then reviewed to identify those in need of individualized academic support.

That review is one of the ways students reach Beyond Limits Academics. See how tutoring works.

What the program is for

PEACE offers a first-rate basketball educational experience emphasizing the development of strong fundamentals and high quality competition, as well as establishing a culture of excellence among our players and in our overall approach to the game. PEACE is built on a foundation consisting of teamwork, discipline, leadership, and sportsmanship. Rather than focusing exclusively on wins and losses, the goal of PEACE includes using the game of basketball as a vehicle through which to positively influence young people within Stamford and other lower Fairfield County communities towards academic achievement, community service, and other life skills. As a nationally elite program, PEACE’s spring/summer AAU travel schedule includes some of the most competitive age-group tournaments and college showcase events in the United States.

The other half of the Foundation

Beyond Limits is our tutoring program.

One on one math and science tutoring for students in lower Fairfield County, open to players and non-players alike.
