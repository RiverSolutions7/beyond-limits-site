How sessions work

Individualized academic tutoring in math and science, generally two one hour sessions each week per participant, available on weekday afternoons and evenings and Saturdays on a personalized schedule. Tutoring is conducted peer to peer: our tutors are high achieving local high school juniors, seniors and local college students assigned to 4th through 10th graders confirm grades served. Tutors and tutees rotate, with pairings based upon our assessment of current best fit. Sessions can take place in person in our tutoring center or remotely via Zoom confirm online availability. Our approach also offers valuable informal mentoring benefits.

Scholarships our students have won

Beyond Limits students are eligible for two scholarships awarded through Stamford Peace. Both weigh academic standing, citizenship and financial need.

The Stamford Rotary Trust Scholarship

The Stamford Rotary Trust Scholarship offers non-renewable, one-year scholarships to students with financial need who display academic achievement.

It is awarded to Beyond Limits students who are in good academic standing or show commitment to improve that standing, display good citizenship, and demonstrate financial need.

2025 recipients: Annabella, 5th grade, and Mia, 6th grade.

Bill Susetka Memorial Scholarship

This scholarship is awarded to students in honor of Bill Susetka’s memory. Bill graduated from Stamford Catholic High School. He was accomplished in athletics, having played on the Stamford World Champion Babe Ruth baseball team and the varsity baseball and basketball teams at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute. Still, he remained humble and reveled in other’s triumphs.

Bill was an inspirational leader with a kind, compassionate, and generous nature, who cared deeply about people and always had a smile and encouraging words to share. To honor his memory, the friends and family of Bill Susetka have established this scholarship for worthy Stamford Peace student-athletes. The successful candidate is in good academic standing, displays good citizenship, and demonstrates financial need.

2025-2026 Beyond Limits recipients: Anthony Lopez and Danna Rivas.
