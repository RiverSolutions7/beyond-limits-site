Our Impact

Beyond Limits strikes at the heart of the opportunity gap which confronts our society’s lower-income families.

Beyond Limits Academics

Established in late 2013 confirm launch year and supporting initially five students, Beyond Limits is now working with over 200 students confirm student count, sources differ in the 3rd through 12th grades confirm grades served, sources differ. Today, we support even more non-athletes than we do athletes. Over 80 percent of our participants are from families eligible for free or reduced-price school lunch confirm eligibility rate, sources differ, whom we charge a heavily subsidized rate. Our greatest success has been the degree to which we continue to uniquely serve this historically underserved segment of the population with:

  • one-on-one academic tutoring primarily in math and science;
  • enrichment programming (including computer coding classes, writing workshops; college planning workshops, time management and organizational skills workshops, and more);
  • summer programming (including ongoing academic tutoring, 1-week middle school and high school preparatory academies designed to help orient Stamford Public School incoming 6th graders and incoming 9th graders, and our new Summer Math Academies);
  • informal peer and professional mentoring; and
  • educational advocacy.

In the course of our activities, we actively partner with parents, teachers and administrators as important shared advocates for our students.

Our growth and success over the years, measured and evaluated based upon data as well as testimonials, reflects the perceived value of our unique peer-to-peer academic support model. Of course, we believe much of that has to do with the quality of our delivery, including the care with which we match students with tutors, our tutors’ knowledge and experience level, the affordability and flexible timing of our tutoring sessions, and other internal factors. Externally, it is also a function of the huge demand for affordable academic support services particularly for lower income youth.

Peace Basketball

On the basketball side, we are anchored by the tenet that “basketball is a privilege.”

We emphasize to our players that the game, and the accompanying “investments” by parents, coaches and other mentors in their success, is neither a “given” nor an “end” in and of itself. Rather, playing is the ongoing reward for our players’ pursuit of excellence in their core responsibilities in the classroom, in the community, and at home. We encourage our players to “use basketball, rather than to allow basketball to use them,” a principal mantra by which we drive student-athletes to appropriately prioritize their academics and other life skills.

From one team of 9-10 year old Stamford boys in 2008, Stamford Peace has grown to more than 1,200 young people today across a variety of basketball and direct academic support and enrichment programs. Nearly 100 of our players attend college as basketball student-athletes, with many more pursuing their studies and playing sports recreationally. Moreover, we can count many of our former participants among the ranks of our current coaches, who are thus paying forward the lessons they themselves learned as part of Stamford Peace.
