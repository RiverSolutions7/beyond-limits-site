Why sponsorship works

Corporations spend on sponsorship every year for one reason: it works, building goodwill and visibility with the community a business serves. A local sponsor gets that same return, plus something a stadium naming deal never delivers, a direct hand in whether a student nearby gets the help they need.

Business sponsorship

Five hundred dollars keeps one student in tutoring for three months.

Every level above that is the same idea at a larger scale, and all of it supports the Beyond Limits Academic Program.

For in-kind sponsorship we will contact you to discuss the details and customize a package for you.
